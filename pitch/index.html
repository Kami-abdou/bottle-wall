How it works

How a move travels from a phone to the bottles

Phone

Scan the QR code, steer with a D-pad. No app.

Server

Runs the game and sends about 20 frames a second.

ESP32

A small controller running WLED, open-source LED software.

Bottles

One waterproof LED pushed into each bottle’s neck.

Same build at two sizes: 64 bottles for the mini wall you just played, 512 bottles on the facade.
